Peanut Butter Cookies Recipe

About Peanut Butter Cookies

These delicious cookies are also deliciously simple to make! With only three ingredients and minimal equipment required, anyone can make this delicious treat! See below for how to make peanut butter cookies.

How to Make Peanut Butter Cookies

Prep Time: 10 minutes

Makes: Approximately 2 dozen cookies

Ingredients

  • 1 cup creamy peanut butter
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1 large egg

Instructions

  1. Prehead oven to 350 degrees F.
  2. Combine all ingredients in a bowl using an electric or stand mixer until smooth.
  3. Roll mixture into 1-inch balls and place on ungreased baking sheet.
  4. Flatten each ball with a fork in a crisscross pattern.
  5. Bake in the oven until the edges are crispy, about 10 minutes.
  6. Allow to cool completely, then serve and enjoy!
